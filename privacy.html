In force from 18 November 2026

Who we are

Taxon is made by Mackenzie Costs Limited, a company registered in England and Wales under number 16170222, whose registered office is at 66 Paul Street, London, EC2A 4NE. We decide how the personal data described here is used, so we are its controller. You can contact us about it at hello@taxoncosts.com.

This notice covers Taxon accounts, the Taxon app, the account website at account.taxoncosts.com and the website at taxoncosts.com.

What we never receive

Your matters, bills, budgets, schedules, time records and client details stay in your files, on your Mac or in the storage you choose. The Taxon app does not send them to us and we never receive them.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name and email address, and your password, which we store only in a scrambled form that cannot be turned back into the password. If you sign in with Microsoft, we also keep the identifiers Microsoft gives us for you and your organisation.
  • Your firm: the firm's name, who its admin is, how many seats it has, who holds each seat, the email addresses invited to seats, and whether the subscription is paid.
  • Your Mac: the name of the Mac that holds your seat, a one-way scrambled form of its hardware identifier, and the dates on which the seat was activated, checked and moved. We cannot recover the hardware identifier from the scrambled form.
  • Sign-in records: when each sign-in began and was last used.
  • Payments: Stripe takes card payments. We do not see or keep card numbers. We keep Stripe's references for your firm and its subscription, and the payment status.
  • Technical records: when you use the account website or the app contacts our server, our hosting provider records your IP address, the time and the address requested.

Why we use it

  • To provide Taxon under our contract with your firm: to sign you in, give out and check seats, take payment and send invitations and password reset emails. Where you are a user rather than the buyer, we rely on our legitimate interest in providing the service your firm has bought.
  • To keep Taxon secure and stop seats being shared or misused. This is our legitimate interest.
  • To keep accounting and tax records, which the law requires.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Who sees it

  • Your firm's admin can see the name and email address of each seat holder, the name of the Mac each seat is on, and the history of seat moves.
  • Railway hosts our server, database and download files in its EU West region in Amsterdam.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Resend sends our invitation and password reset emails.
  • Microsoft signs you in, if you choose to sign in with Microsoft.
  • GitHub hosts the taxoncosts.com website and records visitors' IP addresses in its logs.
  • Google supplies the typefaces on taxoncosts.com, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when it loads them. Google Fonts sets no cookies.

These companies act on our instructions, except that Stripe and Microsoft also use some of the data under their own privacy notices. Some of them are based in the United States or may reach data from there. Where personal data leaves the UK, it is protected by UK adequacy regulations, including the UK extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or by the international data transfer agreement or addendum approved for UK transfers.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Account, firm, seat and payment records: while the account is in use, and for six years after your firm's subscription ends, so that we can keep accounting records and deal with any claim.
  • Invitations: until they are accepted or withdrawn.
  • Password reset links: they stop working after 30 minutes.
  • Technical records held by our hosting provider: no more than 30 days.

Cookies

The account website uses two cookies, both needed to sign you in:

  • taxon_web keeps you signed in, for up to 30 days.
  • taxon_microsoft exists for up to 10 minutes while you sign in with Microsoft, to check that the reply comes from the sign-in you started.

The taxoncosts.com website uses no cookies. Neither site uses analytics or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You may ask us for a copy of your personal data, and ask us to correct it, delete it, limit how we use it or send it to you in a form you can reuse. You may also object to our using it on the basis of our legitimate interests. Write to hello@taxoncosts.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would like the chance to put things right first.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will publish the new version here with the date it comes into force, and tell account holders of any important change by email.
