Questions

Before you ask.

Who is Taxon for?

Costs lawyers, costs draftsmen and solicitors who prepare their own bills and budgets in England and Wales, and who work on a Mac.

Does it run on Windows?

No. Taxon is written for the Mac and uses the Mac's own menus, shortcuts, undo and file handling.

Can I import time from our practice management system?

Yes, if the system can export a ledger to Excel, CSV or text. You tell Taxon which column is which, check the result in a review queue, and then place the work in the matter.

Where is my data kept?

In the matter file, wherever you save it. Taxon never sends your matters anywhere. It contacts our server only to sign you in and to check that your seat is paid for.

What happens if I stop paying?

You can still open, read and print every matter. You need a paid seat to create or change work and to export.

How does my firm buy it?

Choose how many seats you need and pay by card. You then invite each person by email. They accept the seat, download Taxon and sign in with Microsoft or an email and password.

Can I move Taxon to a new Mac?

Yes. Each seat works on one Mac at a time. You can move your seat yourself once every 30 days, and your firm's admin can move it at any time.

Who makes it?

Taxon is made by Mackenzie Costs Limited and was written by a practising costs lawyer.
